# Lorebound: Unbound (Math 15 prequel) — status

Started 2026-11-05. **Lorebound: Unbound** is the Math 15 prequel to Lorebound (the name was decided 2026-11-05). It runs on the same engine with a new map, lands, creatures and questions. The hero walks the road west toward the realm of Lorebound, and each Math 15 unit is a land on that road.

Then attach it with add_repo and push the local repo. The built site (`index.html`, `art/`, `ledger/`) goes at the root, same as Lorebound. Then turn on Pages.

## Art pipeline (no ChatGPT)
1. Import Lorebound art as style references with `upload-asset-from-url` from raw GitHub. Canva rejected `ghoul.jpg`; the treant files work: MAHaGKAJewU (painting) and MAHaGNis8Tk (sheet). For a sprite sheet, pass two references: the creature's own portrait plus the treant sheet.
2. Generate:
   - **Sheets:** "4 rows … plain flat light grey background". They come out with 6 or 7 frames per row.
   - **Portraits:** 3:4.
   - **Banners:** 16:9.
3. Download at full size through Claude in Chrome: on a canva.com tab, load a hidden iframe of `/M/<id>`, take the largest `image-resize` image, and save it with a blob download into ~/Downloads. (`/_ajax/media/<id>` no longer returns `importFileUrl`.) Run long loops without awaiting them and poll a `window.__log`.
4. Pack with `tools/pack_sheet.py <sheet> <name> <outdir> <packedH> <logicalH>`:
   - It finds rows and frames automatically; touching frames are cut at even spacing.
   - Logical heights used: BEG about 21–26, PRG 29–32, MAS 38–40, boss 64.
